QuantX, as puzzle pieces

Each piece has a name. Only pieces that can plug into something have a knob (output) or a notch (input). A knob fits a notch of the same type and colour.

Tap a piece

You will see what it takes in, what it gives out, and which other pieces it can join. Pieces with dashed outlines have no connectable faces.

Every piece is also built to the Layer 0 contracts (Signer, Verifier, KEMProvider, KeyValueStore, Clock and others). That is the shared frame, so it is not drawn as a face. Design spec only, nothing here is built or reviewed yet.
